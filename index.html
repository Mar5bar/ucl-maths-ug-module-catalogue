What do these buttons do?

Year of entry

The module catalogue can differ significantly depending on your year of entry due to updates to the curriculum. Use the buttons above to select your year of entry and view the information relevant to you.

Organise modules by

Modules can be grouped together in a number of ways to help you explore the catalogue. By default, modules are organised by Recommended year (the year that most students will take the module in), but you can also choose to organise them by Level (4, 5, 6 or 7) or by the Year(s) of study in which they can be taken. Within each of these, you can also separate modules by the Term in which they are taught.

Details

  • Deep dependents: When a module is highlighted, shows all modules that depend on this module, directly or indirectly.
  • Descriptions: Displays succinct module descriptions.
  • Groups: Shows the label of any Group (1A/1B/2) this module belongs to, if applicable. These are primarily used for students on the MSci programme, who must choose one of the three groups 1A, 1B and 2 and choose at least four modules from that group.
  • Prerequisites: Lists the formal prerequisites required to take this module.
  • Related: Displays modules that are highly related in content or theme.
  • Required for: Shows which modules require this module as a prerequisite.
  • Syllabus links: Provides links to the official syllabus for the module in the given year.

Select your year of entry above to load the relevant modules. Select modules to see how they are connected as prerequisites of one another, or filter by theme using the buttons below.
